Portfolio case study · ZC Smart Digital

From discovery to appointment.

Challenge

Hypothesis: visitors may abandon healthcare websites when service options and scheduling are unclear.

Design approach

Make the next action obvious, show only times that are actually open, give a reference number at the end, and connect campaign messaging to booking.

Validation plan

Run usability tests, measure booking completion and review accessibility before any real deployment.
