Harbor & Home AI Assistant

Chatbot → intent + lead score → CRM, automated

← Listing website Open the CRM →

Chat with Hana, the virtual assistant for the sample agency Harbor & Home. Each message goes through a machine-learning intent classifier. The chat also pulls out details like location, bedrooms and budget, and a lead-scoring model rates the prospect as Hot, Warm or Cold. When a viewing is booked, the lead goes straight into the Lead-to-CRM workflow with its score. Both models are trained in this browser, and no external AI service is called.

      Model card

      What the two models are, what they were trained on and how well they do on data they did not see in training.

      Intent classifier

      Multinomial Naive Bayes on words and word pairs. It sorts each message into one of 8 intents: greeting, search, price, financing, viewing, availability, talk to an agent and thanks.

      Lead scorer

      Logistic regression trained with gradient descent on synthetic past leads (made-up history for this demo). It predicts how likely a chat is to turn into a viewing or sale.

      What the lead scorer learned

      Weight of each signal. A longer bar means that signal raises the score more.

        In a client build, the same flow would use a hosted language model such as Claude for the replies and connect to the client's real CRM (HubSpot, GoHighLevel, Zoho and others) through an automation tool like n8n or Make.
